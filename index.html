For parents, educators & therapists

A gentle tool for guided practice.

BeatBloom is an independent rhythm practice prototype inspired by auditory-motor synchronization exercises. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional care.

01

Set up for success

Choose a comfortable pace, reduce distractions, and let the child select sitting, clapping, or tapping.

02

Follow their lead

Short, positive sessions are enough. Stop if the child shows discomfort, fatigue, or sensory overload.

03

Notice, don’t judge

Talk about “early,” “late,” and “together” as useful information—not right or wrong answers.

Research-informed, claims-light

What the evidence shaped

  • Timing is visible: the app reports mean absolute beat difference in milliseconds.
  • Movement is flexible: a tap can represent a finger press, clap, assisted reach, or therapist-selected movement.
  • Feedback is immediate: simple early/late/together cues support moment-to-moment adjustment.
  • Results need context: the supplied bibliography includes trials, preliminary studies, case reports, and white papers; this prototype does not infer diagnosis or treatment benefit.
